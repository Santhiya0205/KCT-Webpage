About Kumaraguru College of Technology

About KCT

Kumaraguru College of Technology (KCT) was established in 1984 by the Ramanandha Adigalar Foundation. The college is located in Saravanampatti, Coimbatore, on a beautiful 156-acre campus.

KCT is an autonomous institution affiliated with Anna University and approved by AICTE. It is accredited with an A++ grade by NAAC and is known for its quality education, innovation, research, and excellent placement opportunities.

Vision

To become a globally recognized institution that provides quality technical education and develops socially responsible engineers.

Mission

  • Provide quality education with modern teaching methods.
  • Promote innovation, research, and entrepreneurship.
  • Develop leadership, ethics, and social responsibility.
  • Prepare students for successful careers.
