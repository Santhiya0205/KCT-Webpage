Courses Offered

Kumaraguru College of Technology offers Undergraduate and Postgraduate programmes in Engineering, Technology and Management.

B.Tech AI & Data Science

Master of Business Administration (MBA)

Develop leadership, entrepreneurship, finance and management skills.
