Welcome to KCT

Kumaraguru College of Technology (KCT), Coimbatore, was established in 1984 under the Ramanandha Adigalar Foundation. The college is located on a beautiful 156-acre campus in Saravanampatti, Coimbatore.

KCT is an autonomous institution affiliated with Anna University and approved by AICTE. It has received NAAC A++ accreditation and offers quality education with excellent placement opportunities.
